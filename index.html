Noir Commerce

01 · Installation

Quick start

  1. In Shopify admin, open Online Store → Themes.
  2. Select Import theme → Upload ZIP file and upload the Noir Commerce package.
  3. Open Customize and select the Noir Commerce preset.
  4. Add your logo, store navigation, colours, fonts, social links, and contact details under Theme settings.
  5. Assign products and collections to the homepage sections.
  6. Preview desktop and mobile layouts before publishing.
Safe setup: keep the previous live theme published until the new theme has been reviewed on mobile, desktop, product, collection, cart, search, and customer pages.

02 · Global controls

Theme settings

Brand

Upload desktop, inverse, mobile, and mobile-inverse logos. Configure logo widths, favicon, and the short brand line.

Colours

Configure the dark and light palettes. Each palette includes background, surface, text, muted text, borders, and accent colours.

Typography

Choose separate display, heading, and body fonts. Shopify-hosted fonts are loaded automatically.

Layout and motion

Control page width, card radius, glass blur, animation, page transitions, cursor light, and product prefetching.

Contact and social

Add support details and social profile URLs. Leave unused networks blank.

Cart

Control cart notes, shipping messaging, and optional Cash on Delivery guidance.

03 · Page building

Homepage and reusable sections

Liquid hero

Add desktop/mobile images or video, overlay strength, responsive heading sizes, copy, and two calls to action.

Featured collection

Select a collection and product count. If no collection is selected, the section safely falls back to the store catalogue.

Collection worlds

Create visual category cards with a collection, optional title override, image, and destination link.

Shop the look

Upload a scene and position product hotspots independently for desktop and mobile.

Editorial storytelling

Use editorial split, stacked stories, infinite gallery, testimonial, metrics, marquee, and before/after sections for campaigns and brand narratives.

Video commerce

Use scroll-expanding, horizontal product, and vertical social video sections. Upload optimized Shopify-hosted video files.

Trust and conversion

Add service portals, logo lists, testimonials, newsletter signup, and the footer trust strip.

Locations and partners

Location profile, store locations, and professional partner sections are fully editable and can be removed when not required.

04 · Merchandising

Product setup

  • Add clear titles, descriptions, vendors, pricing, variants, inventory, and high-resolution media.
  • Use the product template blocks to show SKU, inventory, variant picker, purchase options, quantity controls, assurances, pickup availability, specifications, care, and delivery information.
  • Automatic complementary products use Shopify recommendations. Curated bundle blocks can be added manually.
  • Test sold-out variants, products with one and many variants, sale pricing, subscription plans when enabled, and accelerated checkout.

05 · Discovery

Collection setup

Each collection can use its featured image and description. Optional collection metafields provide independent desktop/mobile banner media, heading, eyebrow, description, height, overlay, layout, and banner visibility.

If you do not use the optional metafields, the collection template settings provide safe shared fallbacks.

06 · Templates

Specialty pages

About and editorial

Use the supplied templates for brand stories, campaigns, journals, or seasonal narratives.

Contact and FAQ

Create Shopify pages and assign the contact or FAQ template from the page template selector.

Locations

Use the locations overview and location detail templates for shops, pop-ups, events, or appointment-only spaces.

Professional partners

The trade template includes benefits, process, audience information, and a partner application form.

07 · Media guidance

Images and video

  • Use sharp, licensed imagery with consistent art direction.
  • Provide desktop and mobile assets when the section offers both fields.
  • Use image focal points to keep subjects visible across responsive crops.
  • Compress video before uploading and avoid embedding essential text inside images or video.
  • Add meaningful alternative text to merchant-uploaded images.

08 · Maintenance

Updating safely

  1. Duplicate the live theme before changing code or installing an update.
  2. Upload the new ZIP as an unpublished theme.
  3. Review settings and templates side by side.
  4. Test navigation, forms, product purchasing, cart, search, localization, and accessibility.
  5. Publish only after the replacement theme is complete.

09 · Help

Frequently asked questions

Why do I see placeholder images?

Placeholders appear until merchant images, products, collections, or video are selected. Open the relevant section in the theme editor and choose your store resources.

Why is a section not visible?

Some media-led sections remain hidden on the live storefront until media is added. They display setup guidance inside the theme editor.

Why are products missing?

Confirm products are active, available to the Online Store sales channel, and included in the selected collection.

Why did a setting not change?

Confirm you edited the correct template and section instance, then save the editor. Avoid editing JSON files while the theme editor is open in another tab.

Can I edit the code?

Yes, but custom code is outside standard theme support. Duplicate the theme before editing and document every customization.

Does the theme require an app?

No. Core storefront functionality works without an app. Optional Shopify features only appear when configured by the merchant.

10 · Support policy

Request support

Support covers theme installation, built-in settings and sections, reproducible defects, and compatibility with Shopify's supported storefront features. Custom development, third-party app configuration, content entry, and business operations are outside standard support.

Include your name, email, storename.myshopify.com URL, theme version, a detailed description, reproduction steps, and screenshots.
